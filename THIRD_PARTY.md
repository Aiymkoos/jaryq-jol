# Third-party components

- Tesseract.js 6.0.1 (5.1.1 on the simple screen in `docs/`): Apache-2.0, https://github.com/naptha/tesseract.js
- Tesseract language data: Apache-2.0, https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata
- TensorFlow.js 4.22.0 / COCO-SSD 2.2.3: Apache-2.0, https://github.com/tensorflow/tfjs-models
- Leaflet 1.9.4: BSD-2-Clause, https://leafletjs.com
- OpenStreetMap data: ODbL, https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright
- FOSSGIS pedestrian routing: https://routing.openstreetmap.de/about.html
- Nominatim policy: https://operations.osmfoundation.org/policies/nominatim/

Public service limits must be respected. Do not use the general OSRM car demo to label routes as pedestrian. This application uses the dedicated `routed-foot` endpoint.
